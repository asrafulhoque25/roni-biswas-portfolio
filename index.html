About

I'm Roni Biswas, a digital designer specializing in minimalist brutalism. For the past eight years, I've been challenging conventional design wisdom by creating interfaces that prioritize honesty, function, and raw aesthetic power.

My approach strips away unnecessary decoration to reveal the essential structure beneath. Every project is an opportunity to question assumptions and push boundaries while maintaining unwavering focus on user needs and business objectives.

Based in Brooklyn, New York, I work with forward-thinking clients who aren't afraid to stand out. My portfolio spans enterprise software, e-commerce platforms, brand identities, and digital experiences that demand attention through bold simplicity.

When I'm not designing, I'm researching typography history, studying architectural brutalism, or teaching workshops on minimalist design principles at various design conferences worldwide.

Design Philosophy

01

Honesty in Design

Design should never deceive. Every element must serve a clear purpose. The structure should be visible, the hierarchy obvious, and the interaction patterns predictable yet engaging. Brutalism in design is about respecting the user's intelligence and time.

02

Less is Radical

Minimalism isn't about doing less work—it's about doing more with less. Every reduction is a decision. Every whitespace is intentional. The absence of decoration is itself a powerful design statement that demands careful consideration and restraint.

03

Function First

Beauty emerges from perfect function. When an interface solves problems elegantly, when navigation is intuitive, when typography is readable—that's when true aesthetic power reveals itself. Form follows function, but great function creates its own form.
